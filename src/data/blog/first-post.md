Hi, I'm Chiho Song.

I work as an AI Engineer at SK AX, mostly designing AI agent systems, working with ontologies, and building LLM-based products.

---

I went to Israel without knowing a word of Hebrew. I majored in CS and math at the Hebrew University, and spent my first year studying nothing but Hebrew. After that, I took classes alongside Israeli students. Jumping into an unfamiliar place in an unfamiliar language seems to be how I do things. I'm exploring AI, another unfamiliar land, the same way.

---

I write about three things here.

AI tools and workflows: how I weave tools like Claude and Obsidian into my work. Engineering reflections: thoughts that come up while designing systems. And the things I'll forget if I don't write them down.

Anyone interested in AI tools is welcome.
